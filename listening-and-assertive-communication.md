# Listening and Active Communication
## 1. Active Listening
### Question 1.What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)

- Give full attention
- Do not get distracted
- Do not interrupt
- Listen carefully
- Maintain eye contact
- Ask questions
## 2. Reflective Listening
### Question 2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)
Reflective listening means listening carefully to a person and trying to understand both their message and feelings. We should pay attention to their words, emotions, and body language
- Listen carefully
- Pay attention
- Ask questions if something is not clear.
## 3. Reflection
### Question 3: What are the obstacles in your listening process?
- Getting distracted easily.
- Checking my phone.
- Not understanding difficult topics.
## 4. Types of Communication
### Question 4: What can you do to improve your listening?
- Give full attention.
- Avoid distractions.
- Do not interrupt.
- Listen until the person finishes.
### Question 5: When do you switch to Passive communication?
- When I feel shy or uncomfortable.
- When I avoid conflicts.
- When I am afraid to share my opinion.
### Question 6: When do you switch to Aggressive communication?
- When I get very angry.
- When I feel frustrated.
- When I strongly disagree with someone.
### Question 7: When do you switch to Passive-Aggressive communication?
- When I am upset but do not express it directly.
- Sometimes I may use sarcasm.
- Sometimes I stay silent.
### Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?
- Speak clearly and calmly.
- Share my opinion respectfully.
- Listen to the other person.
- Control my emotions before responding.
