# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What behaviour can cause sexual harassment?

- Unwanted touching.
- Bad jokes or comments.
- Staring at someone.
- Sending bad messages or images.
- Making someone feel uncomfortable.

## 2. What would you do if you face or see harassment?

- Take it seriously.
- Say no if possible.
- Help and support the person.
- Keep proof or details.
- Report the incident.

## 3. Different Harassment Scenarios

- Understand what harassment is.
- Identify bad behaviour.
- Learn from different situations.
- Know what action to take.

## 4. How to Handle Harassment?

- Stay calm.
- Speak up if it is safe.
- Keep details of the incident.
- Ask for help.
- Report the problem.

## 5. How to Behave Properly?

- Respect everyone.
- Respect personal boundaries.
- Speak politely.
- Avoid bad jokes.
- Do not make others uncomfortable.
