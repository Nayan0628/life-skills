# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?


Deep Work means **working with full concentration without any distractions**.

For example, studying Java for one hour without using your phone or social media.



---

## Question 2: According to the author, how can we do Deep Work properly?


1. Choose one important task.
2. Set a fixed time for it.
3. Remove distractions like mobile and social media.
4. Focus only on that task.

---

## Question 3: How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?



1. Make a daily schedule.
2. Give specific time for important work.
3. Keep the phone away while studying.
4. Avoid social media during work.
5. Focus on one task at a time.



---

## Question 4: What are the dangers of Social Media?


1. It wastes time.
2. It reduces concentration.
3. It creates distractions.
4. It can become a habit.
5. It can make it difficult to focus on difficult tasks.
