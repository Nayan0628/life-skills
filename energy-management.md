# Energy Management

## Question 1: What are the activities you do that make you relax - Calm quadrant?

- Listening to music
- Taking a walk
- Playing badminton
- Spending time with friends and family
- Watching something I enjoy
## Question 2: When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?

- When I have too many tasks to complete.
- When I have deadlines or exams.
- When I am learning something difficult.
- When I overthink about problems.

## Question 3: How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?

- I feel energetic and motivated.
- I enjoy what I am doing.
- I feel curious to learn and try new things.
- I can focus better on my work.


## Question 4: Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief. Only the points, no explanation.

- Sleep is very important for both the body and brain.
- Good sleep improves memory and learning.
- Sleep helps the brain recover and process information.
- Poor sleep can negatively affect physical health.
- Getting enough quality sleep helps us perform better.


## Question 5: What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

- Follow a regular sleep and wake-up time.
- Get enough hours of sleep every night.
- Avoid using phones or screens before sleeping.
- Exercise regularly during the day.
- Relax or meditate before going to bed.


## Question 6: Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise. Minimum 5 points, only the points.

- Exercise improves the overall health of the brain.
- Exercise helps improve memory and learning.
- Physical activity improves concentration and focus.
- Exercise can reduce stress and improve mood.
- Regular exercise supports brain growth and development.
- Physical activity can make us more energetic and active.

## Question 7: What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

- Set a fixed time for exercise every day.
- Start with simple activities like walking.
- Play sports that I enjoy, such as badminton.
- Exercise with friends to stay motivated.
- Avoid sitting for long periods.
- Make exercise a part of my daily routine.
