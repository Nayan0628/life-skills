# Grit and Growth Mindset

### Question 1: Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.

Grit means staying committed to a goal and continuing to work even when things become difficult. Success depends not only on talent but also on patience, effort, and perseverance.

### Question 2: Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

A growth mindset means believing that our skills and abilities can improve through effort, learning, and practice. Instead of being afraid of failure, we should see mistakes and challenges as opportunities to improve.

### Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and efforts can influence the results we get.
The key point of the video is that we should focus on things we can control instead of blaming luck, other people, or outside situations. Taking responsibility for our actions helps us stay motivated.

### Question 4: What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset.

- Believe that you can improve.
- Accept challenges.
- Learn from mistakes and failures.
- Focus on the process
- Ask for feedback.
- Practice regularly.
### Question 5: What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?


- I will treat mistakes as a chance to learn.
- I will not give up easily.
- I will focus on improving step by step.
- I will ask for feedback and work on my weak areas.
- I will practice regularly.
- I will take responsibility for my progress.
