## Question 1 In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was that **small habits can create big changes**. We don't have to start with a big goal. We can start with a very small action and slowly make it part of our daily routine.

## Question 2 How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

B = MAP means:

- **M – Motivation:** The desire or willingness to do something.
- **A – Ability:** Making the habit simple and easy to do.
- **P – Prompt:** A reminder or trigger that tells us to do the habit.

## Question 3 Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit? (This is the most important concept in today's topic. Whatever you celebrate becomes a habit)

"Shine" means **celebrating immediately after completing a habit**.

Celebrating gives us a positive feeling and helps our brain remember the behavior. When we feel good after doing something, we are more likely to repeat it.

So, celebrating small successes helps a tiny habit become a regular habit.

## Question 5 What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book says that habits are strongly connected to our **identity**.

Instead of only thinking:

 "I want to read more books."

"I am a reader."

Our repeated actions help shape the person we want to become.

## Question 6 Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?
- Start with a very small version of the habit.
- Keep the required things easily available.
- Use the **2-minute rule**.
- Connect the new habit with an existing habit.

## Question 7 Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?


- Keep distractions away.
- Remove temptations from the environment.
- Log out of distracting apps.
- Keep the phone away while studying.
